I'm starting this blog for a simple reason: the best way I know to get better at product thinking is to do it out loud.

## What I'll write about

- **Product teardowns.** Picking apart features I use every day and asking what they're optimizing for.
- **Things I'm building.** What I decided, what I cut, and what I'd do differently.
- **Learning in public.** Notes from my Technical Product Management program at Northeastern, and from recruiting for PM internships.

## Why bother

Writing forces a decision. It's easy to have a vague opinion about why an app works; it's harder to write down the trade-off it made and defend it. That's the muscle I want to build.
