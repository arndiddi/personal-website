I began playing basketball around 4th grade and fell in love with it. I started by playing with my friends at the local gym, and then at the park with older and more advanced players.

I also played in organized summer and recreational leagues during middle school, and on my school's basketball team during my freshman year of high school.

In undergrad, I joined my college's organized recreational basketball league and also played casually with my friends at the park.

If you are ever open to playing or just putting some shots up, please let me know!
