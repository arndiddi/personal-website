I started going to the gym consistently around the end of my senior year of high school. During college, I stopped going as often because I was busy with school. However, I began going more consistently again toward the end of my senior year of college.

Without any guide or help, I ended up training for strength for the better part of a year before realizing that my goal of getting bigger fit hypertrophy training more.

I am currently on a lean bulk: maximizing muscle growth while minimizing fat gain. The journey is long, but I have definitely seen improvement from when I first started.

Let me know if you have any tips or advice for the gym or for diet!
