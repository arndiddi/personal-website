I recently got into anime this past year. Initially, I didn't like it because I fell for the stereotype that anime shows are always long and have too much fighting. But after watching many shows, I can attest that the stigma isn't true at all. There is so much more depth to these shows.

The first show I watched was *Attack on Titan*, and I can say that it is still the best one after watching many more. The story, the animation, the plot twists: everything is just perfect.

So far I've watched *Death Note*, *Jujutsu Kaisen*, *Hell's Paradise*, *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood*, *Attack on Titan*, and *Vinland Saga*.

After *Attack on Titan*, I would put *Vinland Saga* and *Fullmetal Alchemist: Brotherhood* in my #2 and #3 spots. Both of these shows genuinely changed my perspective on certain aspects of life. I definitely recommend these two.
