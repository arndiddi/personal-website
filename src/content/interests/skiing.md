I started skiing this past winter and love it! I definitely have a lot more to learn, but I would say that I was able to get the hang of it by the end of the season.

I can confidently run Greens and Blues, and I have done a decent amount of Blacks and some Double Blacks. I have fallen a few times on those, so I honestly think I like doing them for the thrill and the adrenaline rush.

I love challenging myself on these runs, because what's the point if you aren't feeling your heart beat out of your chest as you're going down the mountain?

Let me know if you know any fun and exhilarating places to ski this upcoming season!
