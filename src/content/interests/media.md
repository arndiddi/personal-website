I consider myself a film buff and a pop culture junkie. I have loved watching movies and shows ever since I was young, and I have a pretty expansive taste in genres, including action, comedy, some romance, horror, and drama.

Typically, I watch action movies, especially superhero films. I love the early MCU movies that led up to *Avengers: Endgame*. I still watched most of the films after, but they didn't appeal to me as much as the earlier ones. I have also watched most of the DCAU movies and Arrowverse shows like *The Flash* and *Arrow*.

I also love watching horror movies with my friends. The thrill and rush of energy is exciting to me, but I can never watch them alone. I can safely say that I have probably watched every movie in the big horror franchises and most of the popular ones on Netflix and HBO Max. Nothing really seemed to scare me except *Obsession*, because of its different style of horror. I definitely recommend it if you have free time and want to get scared.

After watching a movie or show, I like getting the full context, so I do an extensive amount of research on the characters as well as the cast. That has left me with a large amount of pop culture knowledge, so if you ever want to talk about any of it, please let me know!
